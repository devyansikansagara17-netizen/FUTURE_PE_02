# AI Content Marketing Using UGC Ads
## Aura Luxe Perfume — UGC Ad Content Pack

### 1. Project Overview
This project demonstrates AI-assisted content marketing by creating a UGC-style short-form advertisement for Aura Luxe Perfume, a fictional fragrance brand developed for an educational project.

### 2. Campaign Objective
- Create an engaging short-form product advertisement.
- Use AI to develop hooks, scripts, CTAs, and captions.
- Plan a relatable creator-style product demonstration.
- Adapt content for Instagram Reels, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts.
- Produce a final vertical advertisement video.

### 3. Brand Brief
**Brand:** Aura Luxe Perfume  
**Product:** Perfume concept  
**Brand personality:** Elegant, modern, relatable, confident  
**Tone:** Friendly and conversational  
**Main message:** Discover a fragrance that matches your personal style.  
**Primary CTA:** Explore Aura Luxe and find your fragrance style.

### 4. Target Audience
The intended audience is young adults interested in fragrance, grooming, fashion, and lifestyle content.

Potential audience interests:
- Discovering new fragrance styles
- Personal presentation and fashion
- Perfume gift ideas
- Short-form product videos
- Product appearance and packaging

These are planning assumptions, not findings from a formal audience survey.

**Selected hook:** "Looking for a fragrance that feels like you?"

### 6. UGC Advertisement Script

**Scene 1 — Hook (0–3 seconds)**  
Visual: Creator speaks naturally to a smartphone camera while holding a perfume bottle.  
Dialogue: "Looking for a fragrance that feels like you?"  
On-screen text: "Find Your Fragrance Style"

**Scene 2 — Problem (3–6 seconds)**  
Visual: Creator looks at the bottle, then back at the camera.  
Dialogue: "Choosing a scent can feel overwhelming when there are so many options."  
On-screen text: "So many scents. Which one fits you?"

**Scene 3 — Product Introduction (6–11 seconds)**  
Visual: Close-up of the perfume bottle against a clean background.  
Dialogue: "Here's Aura Luxe, a fragrance concept for this campaign."  
On-screen text: "Meet Aura Luxe"

**Scene 4 — Product Demonstration (11–14 seconds)**  
Visual: Creator slowly rotates the bottle to show its design.  
Dialogue: "Take a closer look and explore the fragrance details."  
On-screen text: "Discover the Details"

**Scene 5 — CTA (14–18 seconds)**  
Visual: Creator holds the bottle and smiles naturally.  
Dialogue: "Explore Aura Luxe and find your fragrance style."  
On-screen text: "Explore Aura Luxe"
