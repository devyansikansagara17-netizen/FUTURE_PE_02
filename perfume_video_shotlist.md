# Perfume UGC Video Shot List
## Aura Luxe Perfume

**Format:** Vertical 9:16  
**Duration:** Approximately 18 seconds  
**Platform:** Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts  
**Style:** Natural creator-style product advertisement

### Scene 1 — Hook
**Time:** 0–3 seconds  
**Shot:** Medium close-up of the creator holding the perfume bottle.  
**Camera:** Smartphone front camera, eye-level framing.  
**Action:** Creator looks into the camera and speaks naturally.  
**Dialogue:** "Looking for a fragrance that feels like you?"  
**Text overlay:** "Find Your Fragrance Style"  
**Editing:** Start quickly; avoid a long intro.

### Scene 2 — Problem
**Time:** 3–6 seconds  
**Shot:** Creator briefly looks at the perfume bottle.  
**Camera:** Medium shot.  
**Action:** Natural conversational expression.  
**Dialogue:** "Choosing a scent can feel overwhelming when there are so many options."  
**Text overlay:** "So many scents. Which one fits you?"  
**Editing:** Use a simple cut.

### Scene 3 — Product Introduction
**Time:** 6–11 seconds  
**Shot:** Close-up of the perfume bottle on a clean surface.  
**Camera:** Slow, steady movement toward the product.  
**Action:** Show the bottle clearly.  
**Dialogue:** "Here's Aura Luxe, a fragrance concept for this campaign."  
**Text overlay:** "Meet Aura Luxe"  
**Lighting:** Soft daylight or gentle studio lighting.

### Scene 4 — Product Demonstration
**Time:** 11–14 seconds  
**Shot:** Hand gently rotates the bottle.  
**Camera:** Close-up with a simple background.  
**Action:** Show the product's appearance and packaging.  
**Dialogue:** "Take a closer look and explore the fragrance details."  
**Text overlay:** "Discover the Details"  
**Editing:** Keep the product in focus.

### Scene 5 — Call to Action
**Time:** 14–18 seconds  
**Shot:** Creator holds the bottle and looks at the camera.  
**Camera:** Medium close-up.  
**Action:** Friendly smile and natural delivery.  
**Dialogue:** "Explore Aura Luxe and find your fragrance style."  
**Text overlay:** "Explore Aura Luxe"  
**Editing:** End with a clear brand name and CTA.

## Production Checklist
- [ ] Use a vertical 9:16 canvas.
- [ ] Keep dialogue clear and natural.
- [ ] Use readable text overlays.
- [ ] Keep the product visible.
- [ ] Add low-volume background music if desired.
- [ ] Ensure the final duration is around 15–20 seconds.
- [ ] Export as MP4.
- [ ] Use only media and music permitted for your intended use.

## Note
Aura Luxe is a fictional student-project brand. Use a real product's accurate details if adapting this shot list for a real campaign.
